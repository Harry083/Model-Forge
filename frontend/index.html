Model Forge

Walk around an object with your camera, then turn the photos or video into a 3D model. COLMAP works out where every shot was taken and builds a coloured mesh you can spin, measure up and export.

How to shoot for a good model
  • Overlap. Move a small step between shots so each photo shares 60–80% with the last. For video, walk slowly.
  • Go all the way round, ideally in two or three loops at different heights, plus a few from above.
  • Fill the frame with the object, keep it sharp, and keep the zoom fixed.
  • Even light, no flash. Overcast daylight is ideal. Shiny, transparent and plain single-colour surfaces are hard. A textured background (newspaper, a patterned cloth) helps small objects.
  • Don't move the object between shots; move yourself instead. Turntables need a plain, featureless background.
